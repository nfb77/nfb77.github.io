Nicholas Forster-Benson

Research Teaching CV

I am a doctoral student in Sociology & Social Policy and Malcolm H. Wiener Scholar in Poverty and Justice at Harvard University.

Broadly, my research asks how material inequalities are produced, reproduced, or mitigated through various institutional arrangements and class relations. My current focus is U.S. housing markets, examining the intersection of local politics, financialization, and inequality.

I hold a BA in Economics and Quantitative Social Science (interdisciplinary) from Vanderbilt University in Middle Tennessee, where I grew up. In my spare time I am a pick-up basketball enthusiast.
